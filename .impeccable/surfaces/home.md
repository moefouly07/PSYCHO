## Direction contract

THESIS: بيننا reads as a contemporary Arabic editorial publication about shared life, not an app full of cards and dashboards; it refuses the dating-app/wellness-SaaS default of gradient hero + rounded card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Warm ivory paper (#F6F1E7) and near-black ink (#221E1A), one committed plum/burgundy accent (#6C2440) plus a restrained olive (neutral/alignment content) and ochre (close/pending). Markazi Text (self-hosted variable serif) for display headings only; IBM Plex Sans Arabic for everything else. Sharp/near-sharp radii (3-6px), hairline borders over shadows, no eyebrow labels, no colored left-border cards.

STORY: A visitor sees the two pillars (psychological assessments, premarital journey) as an editorial masthead promises them, reads a drop-cap intro that states the product's refusal (no score, no verdict), then meets each content type in its own native register — scored assessments as a data table, alignment maps as a facing-column "spread" with no number at all, conversation questions as a pull-quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Asymmetric two-column hero — large multi-line display headline + sub-copy + two CTAs on the wide (right, RTL-start) column, a vertical colophon (stat list: 20 assessments · 14 domains · zero servers) on the narrow column, vertically centered against the heading block.

FORM: User-pinned "Modern Arabic Editorial" (brief-pinned; no concept-seed roll — user overrode the dealt directions with an explicit written brief mid-session).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance. (Preview stage only — full finish review deferred until the user approves this direction for full-site rollout.)
